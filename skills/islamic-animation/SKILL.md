# Islamic Animation Skill

## Workflow
1. Clarify the lesson, audience, language, duration and platform. Default to 45 seconds, vertical 9:16.
2. Write a simple emotionally clear story with a strong opening, three to five scenes and a memorable close.
3. Keep every shot visually specific: subject, action, location, framing, camera movement, lighting, palette and continuity.
4. Write narration separately from on-screen text. Keep Arabic natural and proofread.
5. Generate scene assets with an explicitly configured provider, then assemble and review the complete video.
6. Check timing, continuity, captions, pronunciation, audio levels and export dimensions.
7. Obtain approval of the exact rendered file before any publication.

## Religious safety
- Do not depict Prophets, angels as authoritative sacred representations, or other sacred figures.
- Do not fabricate or paraphrase Qur'an or hadith as quotations.
- Do not issue religious rulings. Flag sensitive matters for qualified review.
- Never ask an image model to render Qur'anic verses or Arabic calligraphy. Typeset verified text later.
- Prefer universal ethical themes supported by clear, non-controversial sources.
